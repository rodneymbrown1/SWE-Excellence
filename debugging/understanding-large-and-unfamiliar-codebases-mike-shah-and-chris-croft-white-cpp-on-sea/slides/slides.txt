Abstract (Which you already read :) )

 Talk Abstract: It's your first day on the job as a new employee. You set
 up your workstation and then download a repository of over 1,000,000
 lines of code. Even more intimidating, the code has been around for 20
 years and has parts of it in legacy C++ and also using tentative C++ 26
 features from various libraries. You feel overwhelmed! Don't fret
 however! In this talk, I provide you a collection of tools to help software
 engineers of all levels understand what is going on in large unfamiliar
 codebases. The audience will leave this talk with a few simple and
 advanced tricks for navigating large and complicated codebases.
 https://cpponsea.uk/2025/session/understanding-large-and-unfamiliar-codebases

 ●   Don't be arrogant, and be always be nice to your teammates.

 ●   "If you don't know, just ask"

      ○   If you’re a senior engineer, make a note of where reoccurring questions may be
