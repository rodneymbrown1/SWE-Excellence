# gdb on the target without wrecking the timing {.chapter data-part="PART B · RTOS LABS" data-label="Lab R9"}

<p class="lede"><b>Goal.</b> Use gdb against the emulated board to find who runs, who waits and who owns a lock, using logpoints and a watchpoint, and measure how much a breakpoint stop disturbs the system. <b>Book.</b> Chapter 20 and the <i>State First</i> book. <b>Files.</b> <code>labs/rtos/gdb/freertos.py</code>, <code>r3_session.gdb</code>, <code>run_session.sh</code>.</p>

gdb shows one CPU; an RTOS multiplexes tasks on it. `freertos.py` adds three commands that read the kernel's own lists: `fr-tasks` (state, priority, what each task waits on, any inherited priority), `fr-tick`, `fr-queue EXPR` (items, mutex owner, waiters). Build at `-O0` so variables are visible.
