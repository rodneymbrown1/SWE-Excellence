# Capstone: four planted defects {.chapter data-part="PART B · RTOS LABS" data-label="Lab R10"}

<p class="lede"><b>Goal.</b> Take over an unfamiliar pipeline and prove, with evidence, whether it meets four requirements. <b>Book.</b> Chapter 24. <b>File.</b> <code>labs/rtos/r10_pipeline/main.c</code>. <b>Do not read <code>main.c</code> or <code>solutions/</code> first.</b></p>

An IMU interrupt (~1 kHz) feeds a filter, which publishes an estimate; a 100 Hz control task reads it; an actuator applies the command; a logger reports. Requirements: **R-1** no sample lost; **R-2** estimate published ≤ 3 ms after its interrupt for 99.9% of samples; **R-3** control reads one consistent estimate per cycle; **R-4** every task meets its period.

## Rules

1. Write your hypothesis before each probe.
2. Use only the tools of the book: the first-look output, `make DUMP=1` and `labs/tools/lathist.py`, `make TRACE=1` and `labs/tools/trace2gantt.py`, gdb with `freertos.py`.
3. Do **not** use `make REVEAL=1` until you have written your findings.
