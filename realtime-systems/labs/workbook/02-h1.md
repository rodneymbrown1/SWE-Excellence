## Part 1 · Does the loop keep its period?

`h1_loop MODE period_us work_us iterations [noise_us]` runs a loop that wants to start every `period_us` and does `work_us` of work. It reports how late each iteration *started* relative to the ideal schedule.

**Predict first.** (a) A 2 ms job followed by a *relative* sleep of 10 ms: what is the period? How far behind an ideal schedule after 300 iterations? (b) The same with a sleep to an *absolute* next instant. (c) Every 17th iteration takes 6 ms longer: which of the two versions loses time, and for how long?

```
cd labs/host && make
./h1_loop sleep_rel 10000 2000 300
./h1_loop sleep_abs 10000 2000 300
./h1_loop sleep_abs 10000 2000 300 6000
```

## Part 3 · The Patriot clock

`python3 h1_patriot.py` multiplies a tenths-of-a-second count by 1/10 held in a fixed-point register that *chops* the value. It reproduces the table the US Government Accountability Office published for the Dhahran incident.
