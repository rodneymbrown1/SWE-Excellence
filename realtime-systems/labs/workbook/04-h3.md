## The workbench

`rtsim.py` simulates fixed-priority preemptive scheduling and EDF in integer ticks, with shared resources under three protocols (`none`, `inherit`, `ceiling`), overrun injection and three overload policies. It also implements the analyses: `utilization`, `ll_bound`, `hyperbolic_ok`, `is_harmonic`, `hyperperiod`, `rta`. Run `python3 rtsim.py` for a self-test (the simulator and the analysis agree).

`rtcalc.py NAME:C:T[:D[:B]] … [--edf] [--gantt N]` runs the analysis and the simulation on your task set.
