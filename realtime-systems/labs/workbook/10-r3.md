# Priority inversion on a real kernel {.chapter data-part="PART B · RTOS LABS" data-label="Lab R3"}

<p class="lede"><b>Goal.</b> Reproduce the structure of the Mars Pathfinder failure, see it in a trace, and cure it by changing one line. <b>Book.</b> Chapter 13. <b>File.</b> <code>labs/rtos/r3_inversion/main.c</code>.</p>

`bc_sched` (prio 6) checks every 50 ms that `bus_dist` (prio 5) finished. `bus_dist` needs a lock that `met` (prio 3) holds for 10 ms. `comms` (prio 4) is CPU-bound for 40 ms every 100 ms. Phase 1: the lock is a **binary semaphore**; phase 2: a **mutex**.

**Predict first.** (a) With the semaphore: when does `bus_dist` complete its first job, and does it meet its 50 ms deadline? (b) How many times does `bc_sched` raise a "reset" in 1 s? (c) With the mutex?
